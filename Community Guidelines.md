Welcome to our community! We are a dedicated space for game designers, hackers, and rules enthusiasts to share, break, analyze, and refine mechanics based on the D12-SRD and compatible games. Whether you are tweaking a core module or building an entirely new system from the ground up, our ultimate goal is to help each other make better games through constructive feedback and thorough playtesting.

To keep this environment helpful, civil, and focused, we ask all members to follow these community guidelines. The golden rule of our site is simple: always prioritize **Being Chill** in your interactions.

# 1. Critique the Hack, Not the Hacker (Commenting)

Sharing a new D12 derivative or tweaking a core SRD rule takes courage. When you are reading and commenting on a rule, be constructive. Point out mathematical quirks in the d12 probabilities, loopholes in the action economy, or confusing wording, but do so with kindness. **Being Chill** means not proscribing a solution or attacking the designer or rule, but explaining where it caused friction or confusion at the table. If a specific rule or guideline doesn't work for you, explain _why_ it didn't work.

# 2. Embrace the Chaos (Playtesting)

Pushing the boundaries of a system reference document is inherently messy. When playtesting new D12 derivatives, you are going to encounter unbalanced mechanics, overpowered challenges, and confusing scenarios—that is exactly why we playtest! Communicate bugs, exploits, and unbalanced features clearly and patiently. **Being Chill** during a playtest means understanding that the game is a work in progress and not getting frustrated when a custom rule doesn't function as intended at the table.

# 3. Stay on Topic

We are here to talk about the D12-SRD, its mechanics, compatable games, system balance, and playtesting feedback. Please keep your comments and forum posts focused on the games. Avoid derailing threads with unrelated subjects, real-world politics, or personal drama. Part of **Being Chill** is knowing when a conversation has strayed too far from the tabletop and gently steering it back to the dodecahedrons at hand.

# 4. Zero Tolerance for Toxicity

We debate d12 probabilities and SRD interpretations, not each other. Harassment, name-calling, hate speech, discrimination, and personal attacks are strictly prohibited. Sometimes, people will fundamentally disagree on how a core rule should be interpreted or how a derivative should play. **Being Chill** is mandatory in these situations; if you cannot reach an agreement, agree to disagree and move on. Hostility, gatekeeping (especially regarding what constitutes a "proper" D12 hack), or elitism will result in immediate moderation action.

# 5. Let the Mods Do the Heavy Lifting (Reporting)

If you see someone violating these guidelines, do not engage them in an argument or start a flame war. Please use the report function and let the moderation team handle it. **Being Chill** means stepping back from a heated situation and trusting the community managers to keep the site safe and focused on the games.

# The Bottom Line
We are all here because we love rolling d12s and want to see great ideas come to life. By keeping your feedback constructive, staying on topic, and **Being Chill**, you help make this site the best place on the internet to design, hack, and test D12-SRD games.
