# 1. Our Approach to Artificial Intelligence

At Double Crescent Productions, we believe in maintaining total transparency regarding how technology is used in the creation, maintenance, and delivery of D12-SRD. This AI Statement outlines how artificial intelligence tools are used in our development workflows versus how content presented on the platform is produced.

# 2. Platform Development and Operational Infrastructure

Artificial intelligence and automated tools may be used in the background to build, maintain, and secure the D12-SRD website and its administrative tools. This includes:

* **Software Development:** Utilizing AI-assisted coding and scripting tools for front-end interface design, back-end functionality, database management, and bug fixing.
* **Site Management & Operations:** Employing automated tools for performance optimization, threat detection, data management, and operational analytics.

All code, scripts, and operational tools produced with AI assistance are reviewed, tested, and validated by human developers prior to implementation.

# 3. Human-Authored Content Policy

While AI tools assist in software development and administrative maintenance, **all content presented on D12-SRD is created and curated by human authors**. 

* **No Generative AI Content:** We do not publish AI-generated articles, document texts, rule systems, or creative prose on D12-SRD.
* **Permitted Content Tools:** AI-powered assistance for content publication is strictly limited to minor linguistic and copyediting utilities (e.g., spellcheckers, grammar checkers, and basic readability tools).
* **Human Accountability:** Every document, work, and editorial material published by Double Crescent Productions undergoes human review and approval. Humans retain full creative direction and legal responsibility for all hosted works.

# 4. User-Generated Comments and Submissions

We encourage organic human interaction and discussion across all hosted documents. While users are subject to our Terms of Service regarding licensed content (CC0) and code of conduct, we strongly encourage original, human-written feedback and commentary within our discussion spaces.
